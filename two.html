Rules

Tennis has very simple rules that are easy to master. On this page, we will be explaining all of the main rules to tennis, accessible for beginners.

Serving

Serving is arguably the most important part of tennis, as it starts off every point. To serve, stand behind the baseline (the back line). Stand on the right side if the score is even (eg. 0-0, 15-15, 30-0, 40-40, etc.) and on the left side if the score is odd (15-0, 15-30, 40-30, AD-40, etc.). Toss the ball and hit it before it bounces. It has to land on the other side of the net in the box diagonal from you, otherwise it won't count. You get two attempts per serve, and if it bounces on the net and still goes in, that doesn't count as an attempt. If you miss both serves, the opponent receives a point. Here are exact steps:

  1. Bounce the ball a few times (optional).
  2. Toss the ball a few feet above your head.
  3. Hit it with your racket when it's just above your head.

Playing

After the serve, the gameplay is relatively simple. Get the ball in on the opponent's side. Literally anywhere within the court, except the side alleys if you are playing singles. If the ball bounces twice on your side or you don't get it in, the opponent gets a point. It's that simple.

Scoring

Scoring in tennis is notoriously tricky, though it's easy to understand after learning it. If you win a single point, your score goes up 15. Unless you're at 30, then it only goes to 40. If you have 40 but the opponent does not, you win the game (a unit of scoring). If the score is 40-40, you must win two consecutive points to win the game. Anyway, once you win 6 games total, you win a set (another unit of scoring). However, you also have to win by two, so 6-5 doesn't work. 7-5 does, but if each player has 6 games, you play a tiebreaker to 7 points. Win by two, of course. Once one player has two sets, he/she wins the match. That's it. These are the different units of scoring:

Point
The smallest unit. Goes from 0 to 15 to 30 to 40.
Game
After one player wins 4 points (or two in a row if the score is 40-40), that player wins a game. After you win a game, both players' points reset back to 0.
Set
After one player wins 6 games (or wins by 7-5 or 7-6), that player wins a set. If you win two sets, you win the match.
